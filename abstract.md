Options mispricing remains a central problem in quantitative finance, where deviations between observed market prices and theoretically fair values may indicate exploitable inefficiencies. However, traditional approaches relying solely on implied volatility or constant-volatility pricing models often fail to capture the dynamic and regime-dependent nature of financial markets. This work proposes a novel, AI-assisted quantitative framework for detecting and evaluating options mispricing in the Indian NIFTY 50 derivatives market through a volatility-centric and regime-aware approach.

The proposed system integrates econometric modeling, statistical learning, and market microstructure considerations into a unified pipeline. Conditional volatility is forecasted using an EGARCH(1,1) model and further enhanced through a multi-timeframe volatility fusion mechanism that combines daily and intraday dynamics. A key contribution is the formal modeling of the volatility risk premium (VRP)—defined as the divergence between market-implied volatility and model-forecasted volatility—as a predictive signal. This divergence is dynamically adjusted using a regime-aware framework based on a Hidden Markov Model (HMM), enabling adaptive estimation of expected VRP under varying market conditions.

To address limitations of conventional pricing comparisons, the study introduces a Greeks-augmented composite mispricing score, which extends beyond simple price deviation by incorporating sensitivity measures such as Vega and Gamma, along with volatility instability captured through a Timeframe Coherence Index (TCI) derived from cross-timescale volatility dispersion. This enables more robust identification of statistically significant and economically meaningful mispricing signals. The theoretical valuation is performed using a calibrated Black–Scholes model with adjusted volatility inputs, serving as a standardized pricing benchmark rather than a strict representation of market dynamics.

The system is implemented as a scalable, API-driven architecture using FastAPI, with real-time data integration via broker APIs (Upstox) and support for multi-timeframe analysis. A comprehensive backtesting framework evaluates the predictive power and economic viability of the proposed signals, incorporating transaction costs, liquidity filters, and risk management constraints. Empirical analysis on NIFTY 50 options demonstrates that regime-conditioned VRP signals and composite mispricing scores provide improved precision in identifying actionable trading opportunities compared to traditional methods.

Overall, this work contributes a regime-aware, multi-scale volatility modeling and mispricing detection framework, bridging the gap between econometric volatility forecasting and practical options trading systems, with specific empirical insights into the Indian derivatives market.
