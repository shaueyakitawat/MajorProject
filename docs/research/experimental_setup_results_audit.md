# Audit of Experimental Setup and Results

**Scope:** `latexcode.tex`, `\section{Experimental Setup and Results}` only  
**Audit status:** Audit-only; no manuscript, source, dataset, or output file was modified.

## A. Overall assessment

**Overall verdict: NOT READY for a scientifically defensible empirical paper.**

The section from `latexcode.tex:1084` through the next section heading reports a different,
larger, production-oriented experiment than the one actually implemented in the repository.
It describes 574 days, India VIX, intraday bars, a four-state HMM, a fused volatility model,
M-Score validation, 57 trades, 901 ledger trades, transaction-cost sensitivity, and several
formal significance tests. Those claims are not generated by the verified research scripts
and contradict the available research outputs.

The reproducible research record instead supports:

- Daily NIFTY 50 index data and NSE daily option bhavcopy data from 2024-07-01 through
  2025-12-31, with 375 index observations/dates and 605,159 normalized option records.
- Volatility forecasting with 314 initial forecast rows and 312 valid common EGARCH rows.
- Corrected common-sample volatility metrics documented in
  `data/research/phase2/phase2_metrics.json`.
- Maturity-aligned BSM comparisons on 189,084 common option observations at a 6% rate.
- A separate daily-options backtest with 13 completed trades: EMA 5, breakout 5,
  HMM-filtered EMA 3, and RSI 0.

The section should not be used as a results record until it is replaced or fully reconciled
with these outputs.

## B. Subsection-by-subsection findings

### B.1 Experimental Environment (`latexcode.tex:1086`)

**Status: NOT CONFIRMED.**

The paragraph claims Python 3.12, `arch` 6.3, `hmmlearn` 0.3, a production-grade
FastAPI pipeline, SQLite WAL, eight sequential stages, and institutional latency
constraints. These are not the environment used to generate the research outputs.
The backtest manifest records Python 3.13.1, pandas 3.0.1, NumPy 2.4.3,
`hmmlearn` 0.3.3, and matplotlib 3.10.8 at
`data/research/backtest/run_manifest.json:1-35`. The research scripts are standalone
and separate from the live application.

The paragraph also names `yfinance` and an implied-volatility root solver, but neither
supports the reported research results in the verified Phase 1/Phase 2 output chain.
The statement that experiments were conducted on a production-grade pipeline is therefore
misleading and unsupported.

### B.2 Dataset (`latexcode.tex:1089-1115`)

**Status: CONTRADICTED.**

Table `tab:dataset` and the following paragraph claim January 2024--May 2026,
574 trading days, 1,674 proxy instances, 2,845 strike tests, India VIX,
5-minute bars, weekly/monthly expiry cycles, and an exchange-feed source.
The verified research inventory reports:

- NIFTY index: 375 observations, 2024-07-01--2025-12-31, Yahoo Finance `^NSEI`.
- Options: 605,159 normalized records, 375 dates, 2024-07-01--2025-12-31,
  NSE daily F&O bhavcopy.
- Positive-volume option records: 346,156.
- No historical bid, ask, intraday option timestamps/candles, or historical IV.

These values are documented in
`docs/research/data_availability_report.md:21-67` and
`docs/research/experiment_methodology.md:1-18`.

The 1,674 proxy-validator count belongs to the synthetic validator documented in
`docs/research/critical_findings_verification.md:45-70`; it is not an independent
historical option dataset and must not be presented as the research option valuation
sample. The section also fails to disclose that the index and options datasets have
different sources.

### B.3 Walk-Forward Evaluation Protocol (`latexcode.tex:1117-1138`)

**Status: CONTRADICTED.**

The table claims a 60-day rolling estimation window, 514 effective days, 57 signals,
and 901 closed trades. The corrected research volatility scripts use the 375-observation
sample, minimum history 60, and expanding historical estimation; the Phase 2 output
reports 312 common forecast rows and four fit failures
(`data/research/phase2/phase2_metrics.json:1-12`).

The section says the HMM is updated “in parallel on the same rolling window,” but the
empirical HMM diagnostic uses a trailing 120-feature window and evaluates two- and
three-state models. The separate backtest HMM is causal and two-state; it is not the
production HMM described here.

The 57-signal and 901-trade claims refer to unsupported legacy/application material,
not `scripts/research_backtest/engine.py`. The isolated backtest has fixed training,
validation, and test partitions and 13 completed trades.

### B.4 Evaluation Metrics (`latexcode.tex:1140-1146`)

**Status: PARTIALLY CONFIRMED, materially overstated.**

MAE and RMSE are implemented for annualized volatility forecasts, but the section also
introduces MAPE, annualized Sharpe, maximum drawdown, DSR, and Hansen SPA as if they
were part of the verified research evaluation. The corrected research outputs use MAE,
RMSE, QLIKE, and a DM comparison of squared volatility-forecast errors. The Phase 2
implementation of `dm_test` uses Newey--West lag 5 and a normal approximation
(`scripts/run_phase2_horizon_validation.py:264-304`).

The isolated backtest explicitly reports `sharpe_ratio: null` and `sortino_ratio: null`
in `data/research/backtest/performance_metrics.json`. The section's statistical-testing
claims therefore cannot be retained without separate reproducible source code, inputs,
and outputs.

### B.5 Volatility Forecasting Results (`latexcode.tex:1148-1175`)

**Status: CONTRADICTED.**

Table `tab:vol_forecast` reports 574 days, HV-60, GARCH, EGARCH, and a fused model with
MAE values 0.052, 0.045, 0.035, and 0.028. None of these values match the corrected
research outputs. The reported EGARCH specification is also labelled `EGARCH(1,1)`
instead of the corrected asymmetric `p=1,o=1,q=1` specification.

The exact common-sample values are:

| Model | MAE | RMSE | QLIKE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Rolling 20 | 0.06834745 | 0.08701578 | 1.50787984 |
| GARCH(1,1) | 0.06965295 | 0.08620244 | 1.51387095 |
| EGARCH(1,1,1) | 0.08291542 | 0.12815526 | 185402337.8621 |

Source: `data/research/phase2/phase2_metrics.json:7-32`.

The manuscript's claims of a 22.2% EGARCH improvement, calibrated parameters,
17.8-day half-life, fused-model superiority, and statistical leverage confirmation
are not present in the verified Phase 2 output. The available results show EGARCH
underperforming rolling and GARCH on the stated target.

The figure caption and interpretation at `latexcode.tex:1170-1175` additionally claim
India VIX, prediction intervals, and HMM regime transitions. Those inputs and outputs
are not part of the corrected research forecasting experiment.

### B.6 Market Regime Identification (`latexcode.tex:1177-1236`)

**Status: CONTRADICTED.**

The three HMM tables and text claim:

- BIC selection among two to five states;
- a selected four-state model;
- full-covariance emissions;
- standardized log returns plus India VIX;
- transition probabilities and expected durations;
- a single persistent four-state regime model.

The empirical diagnostic actually evaluates two-state and three-state diagonal-covariance
Gaussian HMMs using the annualized 20-observation rolling standard-deviation feature,
120-observation trailing windows, training-window standardization, `n_iter=500`,
`tol=1e-4`, and `random_state=42`. Its output contains 234 rows for each state count
and explicitly warns against persistence claims from a single fit
(`data/research/experiment_results.md:113-151`).

The four-state tables are therefore unsupported. The figure's “visual telemetry” is
not evidence of an empirically validated four-state research model.

### B.7 Option Pricing Results (`latexcode.tex:1238-1266`)

**Status: CONTRADICTED.**

The M-Score equation, weights, rolling Z-scores, thresholds, illustrative instances,
and VRP interpretation are live-application concepts, not outputs of the corrected
historical valuation experiment. No verified research output provides the listed
M-Score decomposition.

The applicable research valuation compares BSM model prices with daily NSE option
`CLOSE` values using continuous dividend yield, the NIFTY close, option strike,
calendar DTE divided by 365.25, and volatility inputs from rolling, one-step EGARCH,
or maturity-aligned EGARCH. The comparison price is not a bid/ask quote or guaranteed
executable price.

### B.8 Alternative Option Pricing Models (`latexcode.tex:1268-1286`)

**Status: CONTRADICTED.**

The table claims market-IV, EGARCH, Heston, and fused-volatility comparisons on 1,674
instances. The repository does not contain a verified Heston experiment, market-IV
historical input, or validated fused-volatility valuation result. The 1,674 count is
from the synthetic proxy validator, not the 189,084-row historical option valuation
sample.

Phase 2 compares only horizon-aligned EGARCH, one-step EGARCH, and rolling volatility
on the 189,084 common option rows. Results are grouped by option type, moneyness, and
DTE in `docs/research/horizon_valuation_comparison.md`.

### B.9 Mispricing Detection Signals (`latexcode.tex:1288-1314`)

**Status: NOT CONFIRMED.**

The precision, recall, F1, AUC, confusion matrix, and “five detection strategies”
are not generated by the historical research valuation scripts. The numbers 0.936,
0.209, 0.342, 0.826, and TP/FP/TN/FN 804/778/72/20 belong to the synthetic proxy
validator or unsupported manuscript material, not independent option labels.

The validator's metrics are synthetic: both “market” and “fair” prices are generated
with Black--Scholes and the next-day outcome is generated from spot/VIX inputs
(`docs/research/critical_findings_verification.md:45-70`). They cannot establish
historical mispricing detection performance.

### B.10 Mispricing Attribution (`latexcode.tex:1316-1335`)

**Status: NOT CONFIRMED.**

The attribution table reports VRP, vega, gamma, and composite-score statistics that
are not present in the verified research outputs. No surface-consistent benchmark,
historical IV panel, or independent attribution experiment is available. The claim
that a positive VRP component confirms a variance risk premium is especially
unsupported; the live service's IV-minus-volatility quantity is a volatility spread,
not a validated variance risk premium.

### B.11 Equity Strategy Results (`latexcode.tex:1337-1352`)

**Status: CONTRADICTED.**

The section describes a 57-trade M-Score-triggered delta-neutral spread strategy,
3-lot position sizing, 195 contracts, a 65-contract lot size, a capital base, and
an equity curve through May 2026. None of this is the isolated research backtest.

The reproducible backtest is directional daily option-bar simulation with:

- training: 2024-07-01--2024-12-31;
- validation: 2025-01-01--2025-06-30;
- test: 2025-07-01--2025-12-31;
- 13 completed trades overall;
- EMA 5, volatility breakout 5, HMM-filtered EMA 3, RSI 0;
- gross option price-point P&L only.

Source: `data/research/backtest/performance_metrics.json:92-425`.

### B.12 Net Performance After Transaction Costs (`latexcode.tex:1354-1384`)

**Status: NOT CONFIRMED.**

The 901-trade SQLite ledger, rupee charges, lot size, capital base, net P&L, and

backtest explicitly has no lot-size multiplier, transaction costs, bid/ask data, or
rupee P&L (`data/research/backtest/performance_metrics.json:425-434`).

### B.13 Transaction Cost Sensitivity (`latexcode.tex:1386-1411`)

**Status: NOT CONFIRMED.**

The cost multiples, net Sharpe ratios, net returns, drawdowns, institutional-fee
interpretation, and “practical deployment” conclusion have no verified generating

were not modeled.

### B.14 Computational Performance (`latexcode.tex:1413-1439`)

**Status: UNVERIFIABLE / UNSUPPORTED.**

Any latency, throughput, deployment, or production-readiness claims in this subsection

### B.15 Comparative Analysis and Data-Snooping (`latexcode.tex:1441-1462`)

**Status: NOT CONFIRMED.**

The DM table includes baselines and statistics not generated by the verified Phase 2

## E. Missing information required for reproducibility

1. A corrected results table for all volatility models on the same 312-row sample,
   including explicit treatment of the two invalid initial EGARCH rows and the Phase 2
   fit-failure count.
2. The complete Phase 2 grouped valuation comparison for rolling, one-step EGARCH,
   and horizon EGARCH, with group definitions, sample counts, MAE/RMSE, and the
   interpretation that daily CLOSE is a last-traded reference rather than executable price.
3. Explicit data-source disclosure: NSE bhavcopy for options and Yahoo Finance
   `^NSEI` for the index, including dataset checksums from the manifests.
4. The exact chronological backtest partition and a compact trade-count/P&L table
   for training, validation, and test. No Sharpe or transaction-cost metrics should
   be added because the implementation returns them as unavailable.
5. A clear distinction between empirical HMM diagnostics (two and three states)
   and the causal two-state HMM used only as an EMA filter.
6. If any M-Score, Heston, India VIX, market-IV, ledger, or transaction-cost results
   are intended to remain, the repository must supply their generating scripts,
   exact input data, configuration, and saved outputs. Those artifacts were not
   identified in this audit.
7. Figure provenance. The existing figures exist as image files, but their captions
   describe unsupported experiments and must not be treated as evidence without
   reproducible generation metadata.

## F. Recommended changes

### Critical

1. Replace the dataset table and paragraph with the verified 2024-07-01--2025-12-31
   index/options inventory and disclose the different data sources.
2. Remove or replace all 574-day, India VIX, intraday, 1,674-instance, four-state HMM,
   fused-model, M-Score, Heston, 57-trade, 901-ledger, transaction-cost, and
   production-readiness claims.
3. Replace the volatility table and interpretation with the corrected 312-row metrics;
   state explicitly that EGARCH underperformed the rolling and GARCH benchmarks.
4. Recast option valuation as model-versus-daily-CLOSE comparison using 189,084 common
   rows and the three implemented volatility approaches.
5. Replace the backtest/economic-performance material with the isolated 13-trade,
   gross option price-point experiment and its training/validation/test partitions.

### Major

1. Remove unsupported significance claims and retain only the implemented DM procedure
   if its exact comparison output is available.
2. Separate empirical HMM diagnostics from the backtest's causal two-state EMA filter.
3. Add counts, filters, DTE/moneyness group definitions, invalid-forecast handling, and
   option CLOSE limitations to every reported valuation table.
4. Remove figures whose captions describe India VIX, M-Score, delta-neutral spreads,
   or transaction-cost results unless regenerated from traceable experiments.

### Minor

1. Correct model names to `GARCH(1,1)` and asymmetric `EGARCH(1,1,1)` with
   `p=1,o=1,q=1`.
2. Use consistent terms for daily option CLOSE, last-traded reference, and
   non-executable comparison price.
3. Ensure every remaining table and figure is referenced once, has a matching output
   artifact, and uses the same period and sample-size terminology as the manifests.
